FAIR ROLLS

Every egg, provably fair.

Companion eggs are a gamble, so you should not have to take our word for the odds. Every egg is sealed with a secret the game cannot change after you buy it. When it hatches the secret is revealed, and anyone can re-run the roll and see that the companion is the one the published odds produce.

How an egg is rolled

  1. The game seals the egg

    When you buy an egg the game picks a secret number and shows you only its fingerprint, the seal (a SHA-256 hash). A hash cannot be reversed, but it cannot be changed either: the game is now bound to that secret.

  2. You add your own seed

    When you hatch, your game adds a random seed of its own (or one you type). The game did not know it when it sealed the egg, so it could not have chosen a secret that suits it. The first seed the game receives for an egg is locked: a failed or repeated attempt reuses it, so the game cannot reroll an egg by pretending something went wrong. Your game also checks, by itself, that the seal at the reveal is the one the egg showed before.

  3. The roll

    The companion's rarity, species and four stats are drawn from the secret and your seed, using the exact odds and stat ranges published below and nothing else. Same inputs, same result, every time, on any computer.

  4. The secret is revealed

    Your roll receipt shows the secret. Its hash must equal the seal you saw at purchase, and re-running the roll must give the companion you got. If either is off, you can see it.

What is proven, and what is not

Proven by this page

  • The rarity of the companion an egg hatches.
  • Which species it is, including when your pity pick decides it.
  • Its four stats (power, vigor, agility, mind), each within the range published for its rarity.
  • That the game used the secret it sealed at purchase, and the odds published here.

Rolled privately

  • Affinity and personality are rolled by the game and stay private (the secret would reveal them), so they are not part of this check.
  • An egg bought before this feature has no seal and cannot be verified. In the game, such a companion is marked No verified roll, and so is any companion that did not come from a sealed egg; a companion made by fusing is marked Fused. Be careful buying an unmarked one.
  • This proves the roll was made as published. It does not prove the game picks fair prices, which are shown in the store.

Check a roll

In the game, open the companion panel (P) and press Roll receipt on a companion you hatched. That copies the receipt. Paste it here.

Everything runs in your browser: the receipt is not sent anywhere.

Check it without trusting this page

The whole scheme is a few lines, so you can re-implement it in any language. The server's own tests write test vectors, and this page's verifier is tested against them.
